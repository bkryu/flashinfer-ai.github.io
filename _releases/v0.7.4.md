FlashInfer v0.7.4 brings DeepSeek-V4 sparse MLA decode and MXFP4 expert-parallel MoE to Rubin, and adds a batch-invariant mode for GEMMs and quantizers on Blackwell and Rubin that returns the same bits for a row at any batch size. Sparse-attention models get the SDSA indexer and a context-parallel MiniMax-M3 indexer, Gemma 4 decodes from an NVFP4 KV cache, tensor-parallel layers fuse FP8 GEMM with all-reduce, and the training kernels add opt-in non-deterministic modes and Rubin builds. Speculative-decode verification and sampling run faster on Hopper, and DeepSeek Sparse Attention indexing and Qwen3.5 speculative decode reach RTX PRO 6000.

### DeepSeek-V4 sparse MLA decode and MXFP4 expert-parallel MoE run on Rubin

`trtllm_batch_decode_sparse_mla_dsv4(backend="cake")` runs its FP8 decode route on SM107 for DeepSeek-V4, and the experimental Prims-TS sparse MLA wrapper plans on Rubin, NVFP4 KV caches included. SM107 MegaMoE through `MoEEpLayer` adds MXFP4 activations with MXFP4 weights, `kernel_variant="auto"` selects between the inference and GenPhase variants through `autotune_v2()`, and the Lamport transport of `NVLinkOneSidedAlltoAll` for decode-sized steps builds for SM107. Rubin also gets a block-scaled contiguous grouped FP8 GEMM (`prepare_group_gemm_fp8_nt_groupwise_contiguous`), `batch_deepgemm_fp8_nt_groupwise(backend="cake")` for four weight shapes, the TinyGEMM backend of `mm_bf16`, and an SM107 kernel behind `gated_delta_rule_mtp_ucache_flush` for Gated DeltaNet MTP decode.

### Batch-invariant GEMMs and quantizers return the same bits for a row at any batch size

`mm_bf16`, `bmm_fp8`, `mm_mxfp8` and `mm_fp4` take `batch_invariant=True` on B200, B300 and Rubin: an output row's bits depend only on the input values and the weight shape, independent of the batch size, the row's position, the SM count and the configuration the dispatcher or autotuner picks. Reinforcement-learning pipelines can match the inference engine's logits with the trainer's bit for bit. `mm_bf16` takes a bias in this mode, and `mxfp8_quantize`, `mxfp4_quantize` and `nvfp4_quantize` gain the same flag with numerics aligned to Transformer Engine. The flag requires `backend="auto"`, and an unsupported input or device raises an error.

### SDSA and MiniMax-M3 indexers select sparse-attention keys on Blackwell and Rubin

`flashinfer.sdsa` adds the indexer of SDSA (Simplified DSA), where a one-head indexer selects the Top-K keys of each query token for the token-sparse MQA decode: `indexer_scores` scores BF16 or NVFP4 paged keys for decode, `quantize_write_index_qk_nvfp4` writes NVFP4 keys to the paged cache, and Top-K fusion (`gather_key_sample`, `thresholds_from_sample`, `indexer_threshold_select`, `top_k_from_candidates`) keeps only the keys above a per-row threshold for prefill. The APIs run on Blackwell SM100 and SM103, and `indexer_scores` with BF16 keys also runs on other GPUs such as H100. The experimental `FP8MQATopKWrapper(backend="cutedsl")` adds Selective TopK for single-request prefill with FP8, MXFP8 and MXFP4 queries and keys on B200 and B300. For MiniMax-M3, the experimental `flashinfer.msa_ops` index-context-parallel indexer covers TP2 and TP4 with page sizes 128 and 256 on B200, GB300 and Rubin, and `dsa_indexer_det_topk(backend="cake")` accepts FP8 queries and keys.

### Gemma 4 decodes from an NVFP4 KV cache on Blackwell, and tensor-parallel layers fuse FP8 GEMM with all-reduce

The experimental Prims-TS decode backend covers head dimension 512 with FP8 queries and an NVFP4 KV cache, the Gemma 4 attention geometry, with native BF16 output; Prims-TS attention also adds attention sinks, NHD caches, automatic runtime split-KV, and MXFP8 queries and keys for dense context attention on B200 and B300. On Blackwell, `BatchPrefillWithRaggedKVCacheWrapper(backend="auto")` serves sliding-window and attention-sink calls on cuDNN. For tensor-parallel serving, `flashinfer.comm.fp8_rowwise_gemm_allreduce(backend="cake")` runs a row-wise scaled FP8 GEMM and the all-reduce in one kernel at 2, 4 and 8 ranks on SM100 and SM103, and the CAKE NVFP4-transport all-reduce fuses the residual add and RMSNorm at four ranks for any row count. `trtllm_fp4_block_scale_routed_moe(routing_metadata=...)` runs the expert GEMMs from routing metadata the caller built earlier, for example on another stream.

### Mamba, Kimi K3 and Qwen hybrid models get packed causal-conv prefill, indexed state pools and a fused GDN decode block

`flashinfer.mamba` adds `causal_conv1d_fn` for packed variable-length prefill and `causal_conv1d_update` for decode and speculative verification on B200 and B300, with indexed state pools, fused SiLU and CUDA Graph replay. `recurrent_kda(backend="small-bh")` reads and updates an indexed state pool through `ssm_state_indices` for Kimi K3 prefill, and `checkpointing_ssu` accepts strided ReplaySSM trackers, so vLLM can keep them inside the per-slot Mamba state page. The experimental `flashinfer.qwen_3_5_gdn_block` runs the Gated DeltaNet sub-layer of Qwen 3.5-architecture models for one decode or MTP-verify step in a single call on B200, and the experimental Qwen 3.8 block adds `extend` and `prefill` entries for chunked prefill through the serving caches at any row count.

### Training kernels add opt-in non-deterministic modes, Rubin builds and larger mHC hidden sizes

The CAKE training kernels for Gated DeltaNet, Kimi Delta Attention, MoE grouped GEMM, mHC, RMSNorm, causal conv1d and the chunked LM head come in two modes: the run-to-run bitwise-deterministic packages carry `det` names, with the previous names kept as aliases, and `deterministic=False` selects new non-deterministic packages. Deterministic mHC training adds hidden sizes 5120 and 7168 on B200, GB200 and GB300. The chunked LM head adds candidate-support log-probabilities and a detached-teacher KL (`chunked_lm_head_support_logprob`, `chunked_lm_head_support_kl`) and scalar or per-token temperature, and deterministic GDN training and the LM-head loss kernels ship for Rubin. The GDN, KDA, MoE and LM-head training entry points remain experimental.

### Speculative-decode verification and sampling run faster on Hopper

On H100 and H200, `BatchPrefillWithPagedKVCacheWrapper(backend="auto")` serves batches whose requests carry at most 64 query tokens, such as MTP or speculative-decode verification, on FA2 with a query tile sized to the request. `top_p_sampling_from_probs`, `top_k_sampling_from_probs` and joint `top_k_top_p_sampling_from_probs` split each row across a thread-block cluster, which lowers sampling latency at decode batch sizes. The experimental `qsa_sparse_paged_attention(backend="auto")` runs faster for BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 caches on A100 and H100, and the SM90 BF16 MegaMoE backend adds opt-in compressed expert weights (`wcx_max_tokens`) for decode rounds.

### DeepSeek Sparse Attention indexing and Qwen3.5 speculative decode run on RTX PRO 6000

The experimental `dsa_indexer_det_topk(backend="sm120")` runs the DeepSeek Sparse Attention lightning indexer on SM120, scoring plus exact top-k in FP8 or MXFP4 with 32 or 64 heads, for DeepSeek-V3.2, DeepSeek-V4, DeepSeek-V4.1 and GLM-5.x in ragged prefill and paged decode forms. Gated DeltaNet ReplaySSM speculative decoding reaches SM120: `gated_delta_rule_mtp(cache_replayssm=True)` and `gated_delta_rule_replayssm_commit` run on RTX PRO 6000 in the Qwen3.5-397B geometry. `BlockSparseAttentionWrapper(backend="vsa_sm120_blk64")` runs on CAKE kernels.

> **⚠️ Upgrade notices**
>
> - After upgrading `flashinfer-python`, run `flashinfer download-kernels` to replace mismatched `flashinfer-cubin` and `flashinfer-jit-cache` wheels.
> - `mm_bf16(backend="cute-dsl")` accepts M up to 32; pass `backend="auto"` or `backend="cublaslt"` for larger M.

> **⚠️ Upgrade notices (Experimental)**
>
> - To use APIs under `flashinfer.experimental`, install from source with `pip install --no-binary flashinfer-python flashinfer-python==0.7.4` or `pip install git+https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer.git@v0.7.4`, or use a nightly wheel from `https://flashinfer.ai/whl`.
> - Prims-TS attention: rename `bmm1_scale` and `sm_scale` to `softmax_scale`, `bmm2_scale` to `output_scale`, `*_data_type` to `*_dtype`, `use_packed_q` to `packed_query`, `seq_lens` to `seq_lens_kv`, `max_seq_len` to `max_kv_len`, and the MLA `num_heads` to `num_qo_heads`.
