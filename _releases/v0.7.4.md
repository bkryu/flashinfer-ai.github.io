FlashInfer v0.7.4 widens Rubin coverage to MXFP4 MegaMoE, sparse MLA and grouped FP8 GEMMs, and adds a batch-invariant mode for reinforcement-learning pipelines. On Blackwell, the release adds the SDSA sparse-attention indexer, QK-MXFP8 context attention and fused FP8 GEMM with all-reduce. Hopper speculative-decoding verification and SM12x indexer and GDN decode also gain support, and a first set of Qwen 3.5 and 3.8 blocks ships as experimental.

### MegaMoE on Rubin adds MXFP4 W4A4 and selects its own variant

`MoEEpLayer` runs MXFP4-activation, MXFP4-weight experts on SM107 and takes `kernel_variant="auto"`, which compares the NVFP4, MXFP8 and MXFP4 MegaMoE variants and their knobs under `autotune_v2()` and stores the winner. The Rubin offline tuner accepts the same selection, and post-FC2 routing weights tune on SM107 NVFP4. `NVLinkOneSidedAlltoAll` runs its Lamport transport for decode-sized steps on SM107. The experimental CAKE `cake_mxfp8_megamoe_ep16` layer (BF16 activations, MXFP8 weights, EP16) also builds for SM107.

### DeepSeek-V4 sparse MLA, MiniMax-M3 indexing and grouped FP8 GEMMs run on Rubin

Sparse MLA for DeepSeek-style models, including the NVFP4 KV cache, runs on SM107 through `prims-ts`, and `backend="cake"` serves the DeepSeek-V4 FP8 decode route there. The MiniMax-M3 MSA indexer with index context parallelism (TP2/TP4, page sizes 128 and 256) covers SM100, SM103 and SM107. Grouped FP8 GEMMs (`prepare_group_gemm_fp8_nt_groupwise_contiguous`, `batch_deepgemm_fp8_nt_groupwise(backend="cake")`) add SM107 programs, `mm_bf16` enables the TinyGEMM backend on SM107, and the GDN MTP u-cache flush has an SM107 build.

### Batch-invariant GEMMs and quantizers give identical bits at any batch size

Reinforcement-learning pipelines that need inference logits to match the trainer bit for bit can pass `batch_invariant=True` to `mm_bf16`, `bmm_fp8`, `mm_mxfp8` and `mm_fp4` (NVFP4 and MXFP4), with bias support on `mm_bf16`. The same flag on `mxfp8_quantize`, `mxfp4_quantize` and `nvfp4_quantize` makes the quantizers batch-invariant too. An output row then depends only on its input values and the weight shape, and results match run to run, across CUDA graph replay and across the tactics the autotuner may pick. The mode runs on Blackwell and Rubin.

### SDSA indexer, QK-MXFP8 attention and Gemma 4 H512 decode on Blackwell

The SDSA (Simplified DSA) indexer in `flashinfer.sdsa` selects the Top-K keys of each query token with a one-head indexer for sparse attention on SM100 and SM103, and `indexer_scores` also runs with BF16 keys on other GPUs such as Hopper. `FP8MQATopKWrapper(backend="cutedsl")` adds selective Top-K for single-request prefill, which filters scores before writing candidates. The experimental `prims-ts` context attention accepts MXFP8 Q and K on SM100 and SM103, and its decode adds head dimension 512 with FP8 queries and an NVFP4 KV cache for Gemma 4 layers.

### Fused FP8 GEMM with all-reduce and four-rank NVFP4 all-reduce on Blackwell, and ClampedRelu2 CuTe DSL MoE

`flashinfer.comm.fp8_rowwise_gemm_allreduce(backend="cake")` runs the row-wise FP8 GEMM and the all-reduce in one fused kernel for TP2, TP4 and TP8 on SM100 and SM103. The CAKE `fp4_allreduce` fuses all-reduce, residual add and RMSNorm at four ranks for any row count. The CuTe DSL fused MoE supports `ActivationType.ClampedRelu2`, and the split CuTe DSL MoE adds opt-in `swap_ab=True` on SM100 and SM103.

### Qwen hybrid models gain experimental blocks, causal conv1d and indexed KDA state

The experimental `qwen_3_5_gdn_block` runs the gated-delta-net sub-layer of Qwen 3.5-architecture models for one decode or MTP-verify step, with an SM100 CuTe DSL backend. The Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next block adds extend and prefill entries over the serving caches. `causal_conv1d_fn` and `causal_conv1d_update` add inference causal conv1d for packed prefill and state-indexed decode, and `recurrent_kda(backend="small-bh")` accepts `ssm_state_indices`.

### Hopper verifies speculative-decoding drafts with FA2 and adds compressed-weight MegaMoE

On SM90, `BatchPrefillWithPagedKVCacheWrapper(backend="auto")` selects FA2 for small per-request query lengths such as MTP verification. Query sparse attention (QSA) in NVFP4 selects its default kernel on compute capabilities 8.x and 9.0, joining BF16 and FP8. The SM90 BF16 MegaMoE adds opt-in compressed expert weights for decode rounds (`wcx_max_tokens`).

### DeepSeek Sparse Attention indexer and GDN speculative decoding run on SM12x

`dsa_indexer_topk(backend="sm120")` runs the experimental DeepSeek Sparse Attention lightning indexer with scoring and exact top-k on SM12x for FP8 and MXFP4 prefill and decode, covering DeepSeek-V3.2, DeepSeek-V4 and GLM-5.x. GDN ReplaySSM speculative decoding (`gated_delta_rule_mtp(..., cache_replayssm=True)`) adds an SM120 verify kernel and commit path.
